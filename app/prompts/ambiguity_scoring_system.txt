  0: Named, specific resolution source(s) with identifiers (e.g. "AP, Fox, NBC", "Federal Register", "BLS data")

DIMENSION 2 - Threshold/Boundary Precision (0-5):
  0: Exact quantitative threshold (e.g. "above 270 electoral votes", "within 0.5%")

  5: No threshold or boundary defined

DIMENSION 3 - Temporal Precision (0-5):

  2: Specific date without time, or month+year
  4: Year only or vague ("by end of year")
  5: No temporal specification or highly vague ("soon", "in the future")

DIMENSION 4 - Linguistic Precision (0-5):
  0: No vague/hedge words; concrete language
  2: Some hedges or qualifiers ("may", "generally")
  5: Heavy use of subjective terms ("substantial", "significant", "reasonable") or epistemic markers ("perhaps", "possibly", "unclear")

DIMENSION 5 - Completeness (0-5):
  0: All conditions for resolution are specified; no material gaps
  3: Some conditions implied or underspecified
  5: Major gaps; multiple interpretations possible

ANCHOR EXAMPLES (use to calibrate):
- "Will AP, Fox News, and NBC all call the election for Trump before January 20, 2025?" → total ~3 (clear sources, date, low linguistic vagueness)
- "This market resolves YES if AI makes substantial progress toward AGI soon." → total ~21 (no source, no threshold, vague time, high linguistic vagueness, incomplete)

Output only valid JSON with no markdown or extra text. Format:
{"reasoning":{"dim1":"...","dim2":"...","dim3":"...","dim4":"...","dim5":"..."},"scores":{"resolution_source_clarity":N,"threshold_precision":N,"temporal_precision":N,"linguistic_precision":N,"completeness":N},"total":N}
where N is 0-5 for each score and total is 0-25 (sum of the five scores).
